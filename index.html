PRISM Technical Approach & 8-Step Methodology

Direct realization of Slide 3 (SIH Template): End-to-end data pipeline from multi-source satellite and terrain acquisition to GIS impact analysis and prioritized alert generation.

1

Data Acquisition

  • Microwave SAR (Sentinel-1 cloud-penetrating)
  • High-res Cartosat & USGS DEM (30m/12.5m)
  • IMD Gridded Rainfall & GPM precipitation
  • GSI Historical Landslide Inventory
2

Data Preprocessing

  • Missing data imputation (Iterative SVD)
  • Topographic terrain correction & orthorectification
  • Coordinate geo-referencing (EPSG:4326 to WGS84 UTM)
  • Min-max spatial normalization
3

Feature Engineering

  • Rainfall intensity (I) & 7-day antecedent saturation
  • Slope angle, aspect, curvature, and TWI
  • Soil texture & moisture saturation index
  • Lithology shear resistance & cut-slope factors
4

AI/ML Risk Prediction

  • Hybrid Ensemble (XGBoost + Random Forest + LSTM)
  • Dynamic Landslide Probability estimation
  • Risk classification (Critical, High, Moderate, Low)
  • Risk Trajectory: Accelerating vs Stabilizing
5

GIS Impact Analysis

  • Buffer & intersection with road network (PostGIS)
  • Critical highway severed detection (NH-10, NH-29)
  • Downslope village & bridge vulnerability index
  • Cascading river damming / flash flood forecast
6

Field Verification

  • Geo-tagged photo/video ground evidence
  • Offline-first client cache with auto-sync
  • Human-in-the-loop confidence re-weighting
  • Active learning for sparse Himalayan data
7

Dashboard / Alert

  • Live interactive GIS web interface
  • Color-coded worry level & location pins
  • Actionable mitigation recommendations
  • Multilingual support for NER communities
8

Priority & Alert Engine

  • Risk + Impact + Confidence prioritization
  • Prevention of alert fatigue via smart throttling
  • CAP v1.2 XML output for NDMA Sachet portal
  • Multi-modal SMS, siren & highway VMS dispatch

Feasibility

  • Open Datasets & APIs: IMD API, NASA POWER, Sentinel-1/2, USGS 30m DEM, OpenStreetMap roads, GSI Landslide Inventory.
  • Open-Source GIS: PostGIS, GeoPandas, Leaflet, QGIS for robust, zero-license-cost deployment.
  • Lightweight ML Models: XGBoost & Random Forest optimized for low-compute edge deployment.
  • Cloud-Native Architecture: Dockerized microservices ready for AWS / NIC Meghraj cloud.

Viability in NER

  • Technically Viable: Proven microwave SAR deformation and antecedent rainfall models.
  • Operationally Viable: Works seamlessly in low-connectivity mountain passes with multilingual support.
  • Socially Viable: Prevents tragic loss of life, displacement, and village isolation.
  • Environmentally Viable: Safeguards fragile Himalayan ecology from cascading river dams.

Challenges & PRISM Solutions

  • Sparse Ground-Truth in NER: Solved via Transfer Learning + Active Learning with crowd verification.
  • Dense Monsoon Cloud Cover: Solved by using Microwave C-band SAR (Sentinel-1) that penetrates clouds.
  • False Alarm Mitigation: Solved through Explainable AI + Ensemble voting + human-in-the-loop review.
  • Weather Data Latency: Solved via multi-source nowcasting and frequent gridded updates.
